FORMAT: letter (roll) fold, 3 panels front and back
  Panel widths, outside, left to right: 97 | 100 | 100 mm   (flap | back | cover)
  Panel widths, inside,  left to right: 100 | 100 | 97 mm   (Build | Operate | Hire)
  Folds sit 97 mm and 197 mm from the left edge of the outside page (100 mm and 200 mm on the inside page).
  The 97 mm panel folds in first. The cover folds over it.
  The 97 mm panel is narrower on purpose so it folds in flat, and its outside face says so. Do not let the printer "even up" the panels.

PRINT
  Stock: 300 gsm uncoated, or 350 gsm with a soft-touch matt laminate.
  Score before folding (machine score, never a dry fold on this weight). Grain must run parallel to the folds.
  Optional: spot UV on the three coloured pieces (cover triangle, flap piece, the mark on the back).
  Colours: white paper, CMYK. Brand purple #6608A9, cyan #00C7CE, violet #BE65FF, amber #F5B83D. Ask for a hard proof of the purple.
