Viamagus guest brochure, v2 (21 Nov 2026)
Tri-fold, same look as the physical branding: white paper throughout, the three brand colours used only as accents
(a thin edge line, the mark, the diagrams), one colour per product. No colour blocks or gradients.

WHAT IS WHERE
  Outside, left to right:  Flap (97 mm): "Start with one. Grow into all three." Build / Operate / Hire, each with its
                           headline number (3 to 4x, >97%, 48 h)
                           Back (100 mm): client logos, numbers, offices, contact
                           Cover (100 mm): the mark, Viamagus logo, "Become AI-native.", the certification seals
  Inside, left to right:   Synthia AI (100) | Velocity AI (100) | Select (97)
                           Each panel: a thin colour line along the top edge (violet | cyan | amber), what it does
                           shown as a diagram, the headline number, the offerings, a QR code.

FORMAT: letter (roll) fold
  The 97 mm panel folds in first; the cover folds over it. Do not let the printer "even up" the panels.
  Folds sit 97 mm and 197 mm from the left trim edge of the outside page (100 mm and 200 mm on the inside page).

PRINT
  Stock: 300 gsm uncoated, or 350 gsm with a soft-touch matt laminate. Score before folding; grain parallel to the folds.
  Colours: white paper, CMYK. Purple #6608A9, cyan #00C7CE, violet #BE65FF, amber #F5B83D. Ask for a hard proof:
  the violet and cyan must match the booth backdrops.
