# Research notes — GCC Future Index

Verified 14 October 2026. All six leads were checked against the publisher's own page or PDF, not secondary coverage.

**Rules applied**

- Every figure is quoted from the original publisher page or PDF that was actually opened. URLs below are the pages that were read.
- Forecasts are labelled as forecasts. A forecast is not presented as an outcome.
- Global surveys are not generalised to Indian GCCs, and India-only surveys are not generalised to GCCs worldwide.
- Figures from different studies are never combined into one comparison.
- A signal appears on the public site only if its status is **APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION** *and* it is in the homepage set in `src/content/research.ts`.

## 1. Gartner — task-specific AI agents in enterprise apps

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher | Gartner, Inc. (press release) |
| Title | Gartner Predicts 40% of Enterprise Apps Will Feature Task-Specific AI Agents by 2026, Up from Less Than 5% in 2025 |
| Date | 26 August 2025 (Stamford, Conn.) |
| URL | https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-08-26-gartner-predicts-40-percent-of-enterprise-apps-will-feature-task-specific-ai-agents-by-2026-up-from-less-than-5-percent-in-2025 |
| Exact claim | "Forty percent of enterprise applications will be integrated with task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from less than 5% today." The chart caption reads "Gartner predicts **up to** 40%…" |
| Type | **Forecast** (made in 2025 for end-2026; not a measured outcome) |
| Caveat | The release itself mixes "40%" and "up to 40%". Treat it as a ceiling. The end-2026 outcome is not yet known, and there is no survey basis in the release. |
| Status | **APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**, as a forecast with "up to". Not used on the homepage. |
| Why it matters | Agents are arriving inside the applications GCCs already run, whether or not the operating model is ready. |

## 2. BCG — GCC maturity

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher | Boston Consulting Group |
| Title | Rewriting the Global Capability Center Playbook: Scaling Maturity with AI |
| Date | 4 June 2025 (web article); report cover "June 2025" |
| URLs | https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/india-rewriting-the-global-capability-center-playbook · PDF: https://web-assets.bcg.com/66/c2/16f4539648f0a6a455ce5058f7f2/rewriting-the-global-capability-center-playbook.pdf |
| Exact claim | Web page: "Only 8% of GCCs have advanced significantly across the three dimensions most critical to enterprise value…" PDF: "only 8% of GCCs qualify as top performers, demonstrating maturity across three key strategic levers: market advantage, innovation, and enterprise efficiency (incl. cost advantage)." |
| Type | **Survey finding** |
| Methodology | "GCC Survey 2024, BCG Analysis". N = 150 GCCs; India was 43% of respondents. "Top performer" means a score of ≥ 3 across all dimensions. India's own distribution also shows 8% top performers. |
| Caveat | Small sample (150). It is a global survey, so it should be described as "GCCs surveyed", not "Indian GCCs". |
| Status | **APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION.** Homepage. |
| Why it matters | The gap between running a centre and building a strategic capability hub is the gap the next order closes. |

## 3. EY — agentic AI investment in India's GCCs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher | EY India (press release, EY GCC Pulse Survey 2025) |
| Title | 58% GCCs in India investing in Agentic AI; two-third creating dedicated innovation teams to globalize ideas: EY GCC Pulse Survey 2025 |
| Date | 23 November 2025 (Bengaluru) |
| URL | https://www.ey.com/en_in/newsroom/2025/11/58-percent-gccs-in-india-investing-in-agentic-ai-two-third-creating-dedicated-innovation-teams-to-globalize-ideas-ey-gcc-pulse-survey-2025 |
| Exact claim | "58% centers currently investing in Agentic AI and another 29% planning to scale over the next year" |
| Type | **Survey finding** |
| Methodology | Online survey plus EY expert analysis, August–October 2025, of leaders at GCCs operating from India (mostly Bengaluru, then Pune and Hyderabad). Participating centres averaged about 800 employees. **The release does not state the sample size.** |
| Caveat | India-based GCCs only. The sample size is undisclosed in the release. |
| Status | **APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**, with the population named. Homepage. |
| Why it matters | Indian GCCs have moved from talking about agentic AI to funding it. The question now is how to make it deliver. |

## 4. McKinsey — experimenting versus scaling AI agents

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher | McKinsey & Company (QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey) |
| Title | The state of AI in 2025: Agents, innovation, and transformation |
| Date | November 2025 (PDF cover). The search listing gives 5 November 2025. |
| URL | https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/quantumblack/our%20insights/the%20state%20of%20ai/the-state-of-ai-in-2025-vf.pdf |
| Exact claim | "Sixty-two percent of survey respondents say their organizations are at least experimenting with AI agents." "Twenty-three percent of respondents report their organizations are scaling an agentic AI system somewhere in their enterprises… and an additional 39 percent say they have begun experimenting with AI agents." |
| Type | **Survey finding** (respondent-reported) |
| Methodology | McKinsey Global Survey, 1,993 participants at all levels of the organisation, 25 June–29 July 2025. Global, not GCC-specific. |
| Caveat | The 62% *includes* the 23%; they are not separate groups. A newer edition (*The state of AI in 2026*, August 2026) exists with different baselines. If this signal is used later, re-check against the 2026 edition. |
| Status | **APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION** with the "includes" wording. Not used on the homepage, to avoid mixing global and GCC populations in a four-signal strip. |
| Why it matters | Experimentation is common; scaling is not. |
